# FORP-TR V8 | Single-center psychometric dissertation

**Status: planned; no patient recruitment, scale permission, ethics approval or observed clinical results are claimed.**

## Decision after peer review
The core dissertation is a **single-center cultural adaptation and psychometric validation** of the ten-item FORP-MHQ in depressive and anxiety outpatients at Balıkesir University. Target: **300 complete item sets**, approximately 330 enrollments assuming a 10% planning allowance, **30–40 separate cognitive pilot participants**, and **about 50 stable retest pairs** among 60 invited. The 700–900 person, six-month cohort is expressly **outside the dissertation**, a potential separate project if staffing, approvals and a second center can be secured.

## Methodological safeguards
- Ordinal WLSMV CFA, one-factor model. Convergent, discriminant, content, omega and ICC hypotheses preregistered.
- Transparent green/amber/red measurement gate. Negative psychometric findings remain reportable.
- Item missingness: complete **10/10** for primary FORP score.
- Four-week **aggregate service-count audit** before ethics only with institutional authorization; **consent rate collected only after ethics**. No pilot counts are invented.
- Safety SOP includes psychiatrist same-contact assessment for positive PHQ-9 item 9, with encrypted records and relevant local escalation.
- V7 prospective equation uses beta_1 consistently, with one-center 13 and two-center 14 total parameters; not part of the primary thesis.
- The R ordinal CFA simulation and analysis scripts are templates, **not executed or a claim of sample-size power**.
- Source references include Coutts-Bain 2023/2025, COSMIN 2.0, ITC, Beaton 2000, Eser 2019, Konkan 2013, Sarı 2016, Reich 2018, Eker 2001 and DSM-5-TR.
