# Editorial assessment: MAJOR REVISION

This is a **simulation-informed study protocol**. Treating its synthetic outcomes as observed psychiatry-patient results would be misleading. Even if the user later collects actual data, the protocol requires independent scale adaptation, ethical authorization and renewed statistical analyses.

## Critical recommendations
- **Measurement:** Authorized Turkish FORP-MHQ translation; patient cognitive interviews; item-level ordinal WLSMV CFA; model-consistent omega with confidence intervals; baseline-selected clinically stable 28-day ICC sample. Synthetic alpha/ICC cannot establish Turkish psychometric validity.
- **Clinical endpoint:** WHO-5 indexes subjective well-being over the prior two weeks. It is not diagnosis-specific relapse, remission, or full functional status. Separate independent clinical episode adjudication if relapse claims are intended.
- **Sample size:** Recruit according to a prespecified effect and partial R², not only 10 participants per item. Factor analysis and any interaction require additional precision/power.
- **Missing data:** Retained versus missing standardized baseline difference SMD: FORP -0.329, PHQ-4 -0.349, WHO-5 +0.273. Complete cases can be selective. Agreement of IPW and complete-case does not establish missing at random. Do not attach conventional CIs to deterministic single-imputation scenario coefficients.
- **Culture:** Distinguish family support, social expectations, personal confidentiality and discrimination rather than using Turkish nationality or a single question as the cultural mechanism. Use direct, consented qualitative interviews and integrated joint displays.
- **Terminology:** Replace "predicts relapse" with "conditional association with later well-being"; "validated Turkish instrument" with "proposed translation and validation"; "medication nonadherence" only among prescribed patients; avoid causal effects.
