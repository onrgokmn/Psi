# FORP-TR V5 — OSF registration status and lock

**Not registered.** No public registration DOI, timestamp or ethics number exists.

**Registered objective (proposed):** Adjusted association of baseline FORP-MHQ/10 with 6-month WHO-5.
**Hypothesis H1:** Two-sided test of baseline FORP slope given baseline WHO-5, PHQ-9, GAD-7, age, sex, primary diagnosis, log(1+illness duration), prior episodes, treatment, remission, and site (if any).
**Secondary:** Three-knot restricted cubic spline for FORP at baseline 10th/50th/90th percentiles; all subgroup interactions exploratory.
**Sample:** N=700 baseline, ~560 6-month complete under 20% attrition; 500-replicate Monte Carlo with seed 20261031; MAR planning power ~0.896 at assumed partial R²=.02, ~0.584 at .01.
**Missing outcomes:** Complete-case primary; MI under MAR, IPW and MNAR pattern-mixture delta shifts −5/−10/−15 WHO-5 as sensitivity.
**Measurement:** Independent pre-cohort translation, 15–20 cognitive interviews, 30–40-person pilot; ordinal CFA and 21–35 day stability assessment in a separate stream, with 6-month outcomes concealed from psychometric analysts.
**Secondary episodes:** Blinded diagnosis-specific adjudication; do not equate WHO-5 loss with relapse.
**Governance:** Author translation permission, ethics and institution approvals before recruitment; register protocol/SAP/code/data dictionary and amendment log before main cohort's first participant; no identifying clinical data on GitHub.
